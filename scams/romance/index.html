Romance Scams: how to spot them and what to do

Quick answer: A romance scam is when someone online pretends to love you or be your close friend, builds trust quickly, and then asks for money, gifts, card photos or private pictures. They usually refuse video calls and always have a sudden emergency.

Warning signs of romance scams

  • Very loving words very quickly
  • Cannot do a video call
  • A sudden problem that needs your money

How this scam works, step by step

  1. A stranger sends very sweet messages, very fast.
  2. They say they live abroad or have an important job.
  3. They avoid video calls with excuses like a broken camera.
  4. Then a problem appears: a gift 'stuck at customs', a hospital bill, a ticket.

Example messages (made up)

Made-up example 1Hello dear, I saw your profile and my heart chose you. I am an engineer working abroad. My gift box for you is stuck at customs, please help me pay ₦40,000.
Made-up example 2My love, I cannot video call because my camera is broken. Please send me your bank card photo so I can send you money.

What to do if you get one

  1. Stop. Don't click. Do not click links, open attachments or reply. Scammers want you to act fast.
  2. Don't share. Never share your password, PIN, OTP, BVN or photos of your cards.
  3. Check & verify. Contact the person or organisation using a number or app YOU already trust, not the one in the message.
  4. Report it. Tell a trusted adult. Use the 'Report' button on the app or platform, and report to the real organisation.
  5. Keep devices safe. Use strong passwords, turn on two-step verification and keep your apps updated.

What to do if you already clicked, replied or paid

Stop sending money or photos. Do not be ashamed: these scammers are very skilled. Save the chat, block and report the account, and tell a trusted adult. If they threaten you, do not pay; tell an adult straight away.

Questions people ask

What is a romance scam?

A romance scam is when someone online pretends to love you or be your close friend, builds trust quickly, and then asks for money, gifts, card photos or private pictures. They usually refuse video calls and always have a sudden emergency.

What should I do if I fell for a romance scam?

Stop sending money or photos. Do not be ashamed: these scammers are very skilled. Save the chat, block and report the account, and tell a trusted adult. If they threaten you, do not pay; tell an adult straight away.

Why won't they video call?

Because the photos they use are stolen from someone else. A video call would show they are not the person in the pictures.

Is it a scam if they never asked for money yet?

It may be early. Romance scammers often wait weeks before asking. Be careful with anyone you have never met who becomes very close very fast.

What should I do if someone threatens to share my photos?

Do not pay and do not send more. Keep the messages, block the person, report the account and tell a trusted adult immediately. It is not your fault.

Other scams to know

  • Phishing ScamsFake messages that trick you into sharing passwords, PINs or codes.
  • Fake Prizes & GiveawaysPromises of rewards, gifts or money that are too good to be true.
  • Online Shopping ScamsFake sellers, items that do not exist, or payment tricks.
  • Tech Support ScamsFake 'helpers' who want to control your phone or computer.
  • Investment ScamsFalse promises of big returns with 'no risk'.
  • Fake Job OffersJobs that ask YOU to pay before you start.
  • Impersonation ScamsSomeone pretends to be a friend, family member or official.
