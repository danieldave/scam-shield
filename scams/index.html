Scam guides: the most common online scams, explained simply

Quick answer: Most online scams try one of three things: get your money, get your secret codes (PIN, OTP, password, BVN), or get control of your phone or account. They all use pressure, so the best defence is to stop, check and ask a trusted adult.

  • Phishing ScamsFake messages that trick you into sharing passwords, PINs or codes.
  • Fake Prizes & GiveawaysPromises of rewards, gifts or money that are too good to be true.
  • Romance ScamsFake friends or partners online who build trust, then ask for money.
  • Online Shopping ScamsFake sellers, items that do not exist, or payment tricks.
  • Tech Support ScamsFake 'helpers' who want to control your phone or computer.
  • Investment ScamsFalse promises of big returns with 'no risk'.
  • Fake Job OffersJobs that ask YOU to pay before you start.
  • Impersonation ScamsSomeone pretends to be a friend, family member or official.

The 5 steps that work against every scam

  1. Stop. Don't click. Do not click links, open attachments or reply. Scammers want you to act fast.
  2. Don't share. Never share your password, PIN, OTP, BVN or photos of your cards.
  3. Check & verify. Contact the person or organisation using a number or app YOU already trust, not the one in the message.
  4. Report it. Tell a trusted adult. Use the 'Report' button on the app or platform, and report to the real organisation.
  5. Keep devices safe. Use strong passwords, turn on two-step verification and keep your apps updated.
