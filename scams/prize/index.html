Fake Prizes & Giveaways: how to spot them and what to do

Quick answer: A fake prize scam tells you that you have won money, data or a gift in a promo you never entered, then asks you to pay a 'processing fee' or share your details to collect it. A real prize never asks you to pay first.

Warning signs of fake prizes & giveaways scams

  • You won something you never entered
  • You must pay a 'fee' to collect the prize
  • Very short deadline

How this scam works, step by step

  1. You get an exciting message: 'CONGRATULATIONS, you have WON!'
  2. The prize is big, often millions of naira or free data.
  3. To 'claim' it you must pay a small fee, buy airtime or share your PIN.
  4. After you pay, the scammer disappears or asks for more.

What to do if you get one

  1. Stop. Don't click. Do not click links, open attachments or reply. Scammers want you to act fast.
  2. Don't share. Never share your password, PIN, OTP, BVN or photos of your cards.
  3. Check & verify. Contact the person or organisation using a number or app YOU already trust, not the one in the message.
  4. Report it. Tell a trusted adult. Use the 'Report' button on the app or platform, and report to the real organisation.
  5. Keep devices safe. Use strong passwords, turn on two-step verification and keep your apps updated.

What to do if you already clicked, replied or paid

Stop paying. Do not send any more money or codes. Keep screenshots, block the sender and tell a trusted adult. If you paid from a bank account, contact your bank through its official app or the number on your card quickly.

Questions people ask

What is a fake prize scam?

A fake prize scam tells you that you have won money, data or a gift in a promo you never entered, then asks you to pay a 'processing fee' or share your details to collect it. A real prize never asks you to pay first.

What should I do if I fell for a fake prize scam?

Stop paying. Do not send any more money or codes. Keep screenshots, block the sender and tell a trusted adult. If you paid from a bank account, contact your bank through its official app or the number on your card quickly.

Can I win a promo I never entered?

No. If you never entered, you cannot win. That single fact exposes most fake prize messages.

Why do they ask for only a small fee?

Because a small amount feels safe to pay. Scammers send the same message to thousands of people, so many small fees add up to a lot of money.

Are free data giveaways on WhatsApp real?

Almost never. 'Share with 20 friends to get free data' messages spread scams and can steal your details. Check offers only inside your network's official app.

Other scams to know

  • Phishing ScamsFake messages that trick you into sharing passwords, PINs or codes.
  • Romance ScamsFake friends or partners online who build trust, then ask for money.
  • Online Shopping ScamsFake sellers, items that do not exist, or payment tricks.
  • Tech Support ScamsFake 'helpers' who want to control your phone or computer.
  • Investment ScamsFalse promises of big returns with 'no risk'.
  • Fake Job OffersJobs that ask YOU to pay before you start.
  • Impersonation ScamsSomeone pretends to be a friend, family member or official.
