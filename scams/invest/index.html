Investment Scams: how to spot them and what to do

Quick answer: An investment scam promises to double your money quickly with 'guaranteed' profit and 'no risk', then asks you to invite friends before you can withdraw. Real investments can lose money, and no honest scheme guarantees fast doubling.

Warning signs of investment scams

  • 'Guaranteed' profit
  • Money doubles very fast
  • You must bring friends to withdraw

How this scam works, step by step

  1. Someone shows screenshots of big profits.
  2. They promise 'double in 7 days, 100% guaranteed'.
  3. Early members may get paid, using money from new members.
  4. Withdrawals get blocked unless you bring more people, then the scheme collapses.

What to do if you get one

  1. Stop. Don't click. Do not click links, open attachments or reply. Scammers want you to act fast.
  2. Don't share. Never share your password, PIN, OTP, BVN or photos of your cards.
  3. Check & verify. Contact the person or organisation using a number or app YOU already trust, not the one in the message.
  4. Report it. Tell a trusted adult. Use the 'Report' button on the app or platform, and report to the real organisation.
  5. Keep devices safe. Use strong passwords, turn on two-step verification and keep your apps updated.

What to do if you already clicked, replied or paid

Stop adding money and do not bring friends in. Keep all chats and receipts, tell a trusted adult and report the scheme. In Nigeria, check if an investment company is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on its official website.

Questions people ask

What is an investment scam?

An investment scam promises to double your money quickly with 'guaranteed' profit and 'no risk', then asks you to invite friends before you can withdraw. Real investments can lose money, and no honest scheme guarantees fast doubling.

What should I do if I fell for an investment scam?

Stop adding money and do not bring friends in. Keep all chats and receipts, tell a trusted adult and report the scheme. In Nigeria, check if an investment company is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on its official website.

Can money really double in a week?

No honest investment can promise that. 'Guaranteed' and 'no risk' together are one of the clearest scam signs.

Why do they ask me to invite friends?

Because the scheme lives on new money. Your friends' money is used to pay older members, until it all collapses.

How do I check an investment company in Nigeria?

Ask a trusted adult to look the company up on the official website of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC Nigeria) before any money is sent.

Other scams to know

  • Phishing ScamsFake messages that trick you into sharing passwords, PINs or codes.
  • Fake Prizes & GiveawaysPromises of rewards, gifts or money that are too good to be true.
  • Romance ScamsFake friends or partners online who build trust, then ask for money.
  • Online Shopping ScamsFake sellers, items that do not exist, or payment tricks.
  • Tech Support ScamsFake 'helpers' who want to control your phone or computer.
  • Fake Job OffersJobs that ask YOU to pay before you start.
  • Impersonation ScamsSomeone pretends to be a friend, family member or official.
