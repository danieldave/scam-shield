Phone calls scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: Scam callers pretend to be bank or network staff and keep you talking until you read out an OTP or install an app.

  • A "pastor" or "alhaji" I do not know asked for money for prayers. Is it real?Treat it as a scam. Strangers who call or message with a "vision" about you and ask for money, airtime or "see…

Safety settings and habits for Phone calls

  • Hang up and call back on a number you already trust.
  • No real caller needs your OTP or PIN.
  • It is polite to say "I will call the bank myself".
