Email scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: Email scams copy a real company and ask you to log in through a link, open an attachment or pay an invoice.

Safety settings and habits for Email

  • Check the full sender address.
  • Do not open attachments you did not expect.
  • Type the website address yourself.
