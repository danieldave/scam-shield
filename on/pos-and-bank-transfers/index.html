POS and bank transfers scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: Around POS points and transfers, the common tricks are fake alerts, "wrong transfer" refunds, QR or PIN requests "to receive money", and people asking to use your account.

Safety settings and habits for POS and bank transfers

  • Confirm money in the app, not by SMS.
  • You never need a PIN to receive money.
  • Never lend your account or card.
