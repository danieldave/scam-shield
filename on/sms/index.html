SMS (text messages) scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: Scam texts usually pretend to be a bank or network: your account is blocked, your BVN or NIN needs updating, or you have won a promo.

Safety settings and habits for SMS (text messages)

  • Do not tap links in texts about money.
  • Open the bank app yourself to check.
  • Block and delete the sender.
