WhatsApp scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: The most common WhatsApp scams in Nigeria are hacked-friend money requests, code-stealing messages, fake grants and free-data links that ask you to share with groups.

Safety settings and habits for WhatsApp

  • Turn on two-step verification (Settings > Account).
  • Set "Who can add me to groups" to My Contacts.
  • Never share the 6-digit code.
  • Do not forward a message you cannot prove.
