TikTok scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: On TikTok watch for fake giveaways, "get rich" videos that push an investment link, and strangers who move chats to other apps.

Safety settings and habits for TikTok

  • Limit who can message you.
  • Do not follow links in bios that promise money.
  • Report videos that promise guaranteed income.
