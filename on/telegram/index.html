Telegram scams: what to watch for

Quick answer: Telegram is used for "task" jobs, investment groups, airdrops and exam "expo" channels that end with a deposit or a fee.

Safety settings and habits for Telegram

  • Stop strangers adding you to groups (Privacy > Groups).
  • Leave and report groups full of "payment proof".
  • Never deposit to earn.
