Scam questions and answers

Quick answer: If a message rushes you, asks for money first, or asks for your PIN, OTP, password or BVN, treat it as a scam until a trusted adult helps you check it.

What is Scam Shield?

Scam Shield is an educational awareness website made by students. It uses made-up example messages to teach the warning signs of scams and what to do. It is a learning tool, not a real scam-detection system.

What is a scam?

A scam is a trick to steal your money, your personal details or control of your accounts. Scammers pretend to be someone you trust, or offer something too good to be true.

What is phishing?

Phishing is when a scammer sends a fake message (SMS, email, chat) that looks real, to make you click a link or share your password, PIN or OTP.

Should I ever share my OTP or PIN?

Never. Not with 'customer care', not with an 'agent', not with a friend. A real bank or company will never ask for your PIN, password or OTP.

How do I know if a link is safe?

Be careful with links in messages you did not expect. Look for small spelling changes in the address. The safest way is to type the address you already know, or open the official app yourself.

I already clicked a scam link. What now?

Don't panic. Don't enter any details. Close the page. If you typed a password, change it now and turn on two-step verification. Tell a trusted adult. If money or bank details are involved, contact your bank using its official number straight away.

I sent money to a scammer. What should I do?

Act fast and tell a trusted adult. Contact your bank through its official number or app immediately. Keep screenshots of the messages. Report the account on the platform where it happened. It is not your fault: scammers are skilled tricksters.

How do I report a scam?

Open the Report Center from the menu. Fill in where you got the message, what kind of scam it looked like, what you did, and paste the message. It is saved in your profile on this device. Then tell a trusted adult, use the 'Report' or 'Block' button on the app where you got it, and report to the real organisation it pretended to be.

Why should I log in?

Logging in creates a profile on this device, so your points, stars and reports are kept under your name. Several students can share one laptop, each with their own profile. It is a practice login: it works offline and nothing is sent anywhere, so never use a password you use for a real account.

How do I get 5 stars?

Every 100 points gives you a star. At 500 points you get all 5 stars, and your 5-Star Scam Shield award is saved in your profile with the date you earned it.

I forgot my Scam Shield password. What now?

Because everything is saved only on this device, there is no email reset. Create a new profile, or ask your teacher to help. This is one reason never to use a real password here.

How does the Verify page work?

You paste a message. Our code looks for common warning signs, like requests for PINs or money, deadlines, prizes and links, and compares the message with our made-up examples. It gives a learning result, not a guarantee. Always check with a trusted adult.

Does Scam Shield save or send my data?

No. Scam Shield works offline. Nothing is sent to the internet. Profiles, points and reports are saved only on this device, and phone numbers and email addresses inside reports are hidden before saving. Passwords are stored scrambled, never as plain text. You can delete your reports at any time.

What is a romance scam?

A scammer pretends to like or love you online. They build trust fast, avoid video calls, and then ask for money, gifts or photos. Never send money or private photos to someone you only know online.

How do I spot a fake job or scholarship?

Watch out if they ask you to pay a fee, promise very big pay for easy work, or only talk on chat. Real employers and scholarships do not ask you to pay to be chosen.

How do I shop online safely?

Be careful of prices far below normal, sellers who want payment to a personal account first, and sellers who refuse pay-on-delivery or calls. Ask an adult to help you check the seller.

How do I spot an investment scam?

Big warning signs: 'guaranteed' profit, money doubling fast, pressure to join today, and needing to invite friends before you can withdraw.

What is two-step verification?

It is a second lock on your account. After your password, you also confirm with a code or the app. A stolen password alone is then not enough.

How do I make a strong password?

Use a long passphrase of 3 or 4 random words, add numbers or symbols, and use a different password for each account. A password manager can help.

How does Knowledge Earn work?

Every correct quiz answer and every red flag you find on Spot It earns points, and each report earns 2. Points unlock badges (Scam Spotter, Smart Checker, Shield Guardian, 5-Star Shield) and stars: one star for every 100 points, 5 stars at 500. Your points stay on this device, in your profile.

How long does it take to learn this?

Learning to spot scams is a habit you build over time, not something you finish in one day. Practise a little every week, and keep up as scammers change their tricks.

Does Scam Shield work without internet?

Yes. Once the files are on your device, everything works offline.

Are the example messages real?

No. Every example is made up for learning. The names, numbers and links are fictional, and no real company or bank is named.

Is every suspicious-looking message a scam?

No. Some real messages look odd. The safe habit is the same: stop, check who sent it, and verify using a contact you already trust before you act.
