      After you report

      1. Tell a trusted adult: a parent, guardian or teacher. Show them your report.
      2. Block and report the sender inside the app where you got it (most apps have a Report or Block button).
      3. If money or bank details were involved, call your bank on the number on the back of your card, or use its official app, straight away.
      4. Keep the evidence: take screenshots before you delete anything.
      5. Change your password if you typed it anywhere, and turn on two-step verification.

      Being tricked is never your fault. Reporting quickly helps protect you and others.
