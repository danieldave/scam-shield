What is advance-fee fraud (419)?

Quick answer: Advance-fee fraud is any scam where you are asked to pay a small fee first to receive something bigger that never comes.

In Nigeria it is often called "419". Prizes, jobs, loans and grants that need a fee first all belong here.

See it in a real situation

  • I got a message that I won a promo I never entered. Is it real?No. You cannot win a promo you never entered. The message is bait to get a "claim fee" or your bank details.…
