Put the free scam checker on your website

Quick answer: copy one line of code into your website and your visitors can check suspicious messages without leaving your page. It is free, it shows no adverts, and the message never leaves the visitor's phone.

1. Copy this code

<iframe src="https://scam-shield-blond.vercel.app/embed" title="Scam Shield message checker" width="100%" height="560" style="border:0;max-width:520px" loading="lazy"></iframe>

2. Paste it where you want the checker

It works in WordPress (Custom HTML block), Blogger, Wix, Google Sites and any HTML page.

See it working

Who is it for?

  • Schools: on the school website or e-learning page.
  • Banks, fintechs and cooperatives: on the "security" or "contact us" page.
  • Churches, mosques and community groups: on the announcements page.
  • News sites and NGOs: beside stories about fraud.

The embedded checker uses our offline red-flag list only. It is a learning tool: it can miss scams and it can be wrong.
