Scam Drill: scam or safe?

How it works: you get 10 messages. Six are scams, four are safe, in a random order. Tap SCAM or SAFE. After each answer you see the warning signs. Nothing you do here is saved or sent.

Every message is made up for practice. Teachers: put this on the board and let the class vote on each message.

Written by students of the ByteBusters Coding Club, Yewa College, Ilaro. Last updated 2026-11-16. All example messages are made up for learning.
