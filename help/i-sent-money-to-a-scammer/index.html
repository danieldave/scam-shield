I sent money to a scammer. Can I get it back?

Quick answer: Sometimes, if you act fast. Banks can try to stop or trace a transfer when it is reported quickly, but there is no guarantee. Report it today.

Do these steps in order

  1. Tell a trusted adult now.
  2. Contact your bank through its official channel and report the transfer as fraud. Give the date, amount and the account it went to.
  3. Keep every screenshot: the chat, the account details and the receipt.
  4. An adult can also report to the police, and to the bank regulator's consumer complaints channel if the bank does not help.
  5. Do not pay anyone who promises to "recover" your money for a fee. That is a second scam.

Remember: never use a phone number or link that came in the scam message. Use the number on the back of the bank card or the official app. Nobody honest charges a fee to "recover" stolen money.
