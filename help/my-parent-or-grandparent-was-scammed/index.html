My parent or grandparent was scammed. How can I help?

Quick answer: Be kind first, then help them report quickly. Scams work on clever people every day, and shame makes people hide it and lose more.

Do these steps in order

  1. Say clearly that it is not their fault.
  2. Help them contact the bank on the number on the back of the card or the official app.
  3. Help them change passwords and PINs.
  4. Collect the evidence: messages, numbers, account details.
  5. Show them how to check a message on Scam Shield next time, and agree on a family rule: "we call each other before we send money".

Remember: never use a phone number or link that came in the scam message. Use the number on the back of the bank card or the official app. Nobody honest charges a fee to "recover" stolen money.
