I clicked a scam link. What should I do?

Quick answer: Clicking alone is usually not the end of the world. The danger is what you typed or installed after. Close the page, then follow these steps.

Do these steps in order

  1. Close the tab. Do not type anything else into that page.
  2. If you typed a password, change it now from the real app, and anywhere else you use the same password.
  3. If you typed bank or card details, tell an adult and contact the bank at once.
  4. If an app was installed, delete it and restart the phone.
  5. Turn on two-step verification on your important accounts.

Remember: never use a phone number or link that came in the scam message. Use the number on the back of the bank card or the official app. Nobody honest charges a fee to "recover" stolen money.
