Someone made a fake account with my name and photos. What do I do?

Quick answer: Report the fake profile inside the app as "pretending to be me". Ask friends to report it too, and warn your contacts.

Do these steps in order

  1. Take screenshots of the fake profile and its username.
  2. Use the app's Report button and choose impersonation.
  3. Ask friends and family to report the same account.
  4. Post or tell your contacts that the account is fake and they should not send money.
  5. Make your real account private and review who can see your photos.

Remember: never use a phone number or link that came in the scam message. Use the number on the back of the bank card or the official app. Nobody honest charges a fee to "recover" stolen money.
