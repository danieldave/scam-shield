Warning sign: 'It's me', new number, or pretends to be an official

Quick answer: Anyone can type "it is me" or copy a logo. Check using a number or contact you already had before the message arrived.

Words our checker looks for

new number · changed my number · it's me · it is me · official · head office · customer care · agent · exam office · police · federal government · fg · government of nigeria · minister · ministry · cbn · central bank · efcc
