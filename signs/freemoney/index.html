Warning sign: Free money from 'government' or a big company

Quick answer: Governments and big companies do not hand out money through forwarded messages. Real programmes are announced on official websites and have checks.

Words our checker looks for

giving out · cash transfer · cash grant · grant · palliative · empowerment · relief fund · free money · all nigerians · every nigerian · get yours · claim yours · apply here · i just got mine · i got mine · i have received mine
