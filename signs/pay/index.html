Warning sign: Asks you to pay or send money

Quick answer: Scams need your money to move before you have time to check. Real prizes, jobs, loans and grants do not ask you to pay first.

Words our checker looks for

pay · fee · send money · transfer to · transfer the · make a transfer · processing · registration · deposit · gift card · recharge card · send airtime · buy airtime · send ₦ · pay ₦ · to this account · payment before delivery · send any amount

Our checker is careful: it only counts these words when the same sentence is asking you for them. "No BVN required" is a promise, not a request.
