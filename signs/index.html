Warning signs

Quick answer: Scam messages repeat the same tricks. Learn the warning signs our checker looks for and you can spot a scam you have never seen before.

  • Asks for PIN, password, OTP or codes
  • Asks you to pay or send money
  • Rushes you with a deadline or a threat
  • Prize, win or something free
  • Free money from 'government' or a big company
  • Says money was 'sent': check your OWN bank app
  • Asks you to share or forward it to others
  • Too good to be true (big money, no risk)
  • Contains a link to click
  • Asks you to keep it secret
  • 'It's me', new number, or pretends to be an official
  • Wants to install an app or control your device
  • Love or friendship that quickly turns to money
